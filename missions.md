<section aria-labelledby="what-we-build">
  <h2 id="what-we-build">What we have built</h2>
  <p>More than twenty years ago, Hana began going on construction missions in poor neighborhoods of Baja California. Since then we have built:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Bunk beds for families who were sleeping on dirt floors, as many as 70 to 80 beds in a single three-day trip</li>
    <li>A rehabilitation center</li>
    <li>Shelters for people leaving prison</li>
    <li>School buildings that also serve as churches</li>
  </ul>
  <p>In all, more than fifty buildings.</p>
</section>

<section aria-labelledby="how-we-build">
  <h2 id="how-we-build">How we build</h2>
  <p>We bring recycled building materials from the United States. We teach construction skills to local workers. Small churches work together with us, and some of them have gone on to start programs of their own.</p>
</section>

<section aria-labelledby="what-we-learned">
  <h2 id="what-we-learned">What we have learned</h2>
  <p>Two sayings grew out of these trips:</p>
  <ul class="sayings">
    <li>“Work is prayer.”</li>
    <li>“Give, and then forget that you gave.”</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section aria-labelledby="generations">
  <h2 id="generations">Every generation</h2>
  <p>Children brought the coins they had saved for the mission. Some of the children who grew up on these trips now come back as young adults to build with us.</p>
</section>
