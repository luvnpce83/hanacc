Hana (하나) means one.

“…for all of you are one in Christ Jesus.”

Galatians 3:28

In May 1997, five people gathered for worship. The habits of those early years still shape us: read the Bible slowly and let it speak first. Give, and then forget that you gave. Count honest work as prayer. Children who grew up on our mission trips now join them as young adults, and every generation worships side by side.
